What's in it

Nothing fancy — just the paperwork side of a shoot, handled.

Model & property releases

Adult and minor model releases, plus a property release, formatted the way stock platforms expect them.

Sign right there

Everyone signs on the phone screen — photographer, subject, guardian if needed, witness if you use one.

Back it up

Push a copy to Google Drive, Dropbox, or iCloud so it's not just sitting on one device.

Stays on your device

No account on our end holding your contracts — they live on your phone and in whatever cloud you connect.
